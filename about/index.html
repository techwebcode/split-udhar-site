Our Mission

About SplitUdhar

A smarter, simpler way to track shared expenses and informal lending.

What is SplitUdhar?

SplitUdhar is a mobile-first personal finance utility designed to solve the two most common ways friends, flatmates, and colleagues exchange money: 1-on-1 personal lending/borrowing ("Udhar") and multi-person group expense splitting.

The Business Problem We Solved

When analyzing everyday personal finance tools, we identified a major gap in the market:

  • Traditional Group Splitters (e.g. Splitwise): Excel at multi-person dining and trips, but are awkward and over-engineered when you simply lend ₹1,000 cash to a friend for an emergency or cover a colleague's chai tab.
  • Merchant Credit Books (e.g. Khatabook): Geared towards small shops and retail businesses, carrying complicated business accounting overhead that doesn't fit friendly relationships.

SplitUdhar unites both workflows into a single, intuitive interface. Whether you're tracking informal personal loans with "You Gave" (Lent) and "You Got" (Received) buttons, or dividing vacation costs among 6 friends, SplitUdhar automatically unifies them into one single net balance per person.

The Two Core Pillars

  • Pillar 1: 1-on-1 Personal Udhar Ledger: Directly log money you lent or borrowed with any contact in under 3 seconds. View an individual ledger of every transaction, partial payment, and settle up with complete clarity.
  • Pillar 2: Group Expense Splitting: Create collaborative groups for roommates, road trips, weekend parties, or office lunches. Split bills equally, by custom amounts, or shares, backed by an automated debt simplification engine.
  • The Smart Netting Engine: If you lent Rahul ₹1,000 personally, and he later paid for a group dinner where your share was ₹400, SplitUdhar automatically calculates that Rahul owes you ₹600. Zero redundant payments.

Our Product Principles

  • Zero Clutter, Zero Math Headaches: Log an expense in 3 seconds, let the app calculate the net position, and eliminate awkward money follow-ups with mutual transparency.
  • Privacy & Security by Default: Device biometric lock (fingerprint/face recognition), encrypted cloud synchronization (TLS/HTTPS), and zero sale of personal data to third parties.
  • Fairness & Transparency: Real-time balance calculations ensure both parties always have exact visibility into who owes whom.
