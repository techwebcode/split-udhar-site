About SplitUdhar

A simple way to manage personal dues and shared expenses.

What is SplitUdhar?

SplitUdhar is a personal finance utility designed to help users keep track of money they have lent, borrowed, or shared with other people. It is intended to make everyday expense and debt tracking easier and more transparent.

Why SplitUdhar?

Small loans, shared meals, trips, household expenses and informal payments can quickly become difficult to remember. SplitUdhar brings those records into one place so you can review balances and transaction history whenever you need them.

Our approach

We aim to keep the experience straightforward: add a person, record a transaction, review the balance and settle when the amount is paid.

Important note

SplitUdhar is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide financial, investment, lending or legal advice, and users are responsible for the accuracy of the information they enter.
