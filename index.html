The Dual-Engine Advantage

Not Just an Expense Splitter.
Two Powerful Modes in One App.

Most apps only split group bills or force you into complicated business bookkeeping. SplitUdhar is architected for the two ways you actually share money in daily life.

🤝 Mode 1: 1-on-1 Personal Udhar

Personal Lending & Borrowing

Record informal peer loans, borrowed money, or payments made for a friend ("Maine Diya / Maine Liya") without creating an unwanted group.

Instant "You Gave" & "You Got" logging: Add cash loans, emergency peer advances, or chai/coffee tabs in 3 seconds.
Private running friend ledger: A dedicated statement for each friend showing dates, notes, and partial repayments.
No awkward reminders: Keep a clear, mutual record that removes memory lapses and eliminates awkward follow-up chats.

Smart Group Bill Splitting

Manage shared expenses for apartment flatmates, road trips, weekend parties, and office colleague lunches where multiple people pay.

The Magic Formula

Smart Netting: 1-on-1 Udhar & Groups Connected

Other apps isolate group bills from personal lending. SplitUdhar combines both into one unified net balance per friend. If you lent Rahul ₹1,000 cash, and he paid ₹800 for your group dinner (your share ₹400), SplitUdhar automatically calculates: Rahul owes you ₹600.
