Data Control & User Rights

Delete Your SplitUdhar Account & Data

Google Play Compliance: Account & Data Deletion Portal • Support: support@techwebcode.in

At SplitUdhar, we respect your right to control your personal data. If you decide that you no longer wish to use the service, you can permanently delete your SplitUdhar account and all associated personal and financial data.

⚠️
Permanent Action Warning Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, your login credentials, personal profile, individual expense records, group entries, and balance history cannot be recovered.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

If you currently have the SplitUdhar app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the SplitUdhar app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Security section.
  4. Tap on Delete Account Permanently.
  5. Read the confirmation prompt and tap Delete Permanently to confirm.

Your account session is terminated immediately and your account deletion is processed right away.

Method 2: Web-Based Deletion Request (Without App)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your mobile device, you can request account deletion using our web portal below or by sending an email directly to support@techwebcode.in.

3. What Data Is Deleted?

When your deletion request is processed, the following categories of data are permanently deleted from our servers:

  • User Profile: Your full name, registered email address, authentication ID, and profile picture.
  • Financial & Transaction Records: All personal lending and borrowing ("Udhar") records created under your account.
  • Group Expenses & Ledgers: Any expense allocations, notes, and individual split records tied to your profile.
  • Device & Notification Data: Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens) and active session credentials.

4. What Data May Be Retained & Retention Period

  • Shared Group History: If you participated in a shared group with other users, those other users will see that an expense occurred for audit continuity, but your personal identifiers will be disassociated and anonymized.
  • Backup Purge Window: Data removed from active databases is completely cleared from encrypted routine database backups within 30 days.
  • Legal & Fraud Prevention: In rare cases, limited technical logs or transactional audit records may be retained only if strictly required by applicable Indian legal statutes or regulatory directives.
