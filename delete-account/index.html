Delete Your SplitUdhar Account

Account deletion request

If you no longer want to use SplitUdhar, you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

Before requesting deletion

Account deletion may permanently remove your profile and app records associated with the account. Make sure you have saved anything you may need before submitting a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the SplitUdhar app and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the account/profile settings.
  3. Select the account deletion option and follow the confirmation steps.

If the in-app deletion option is unavailable, use the support/contact channel provided by the app and include the account information needed to identify your account. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

What happens after deletion?

We will process the deletion request according to the service's data-retention requirements. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate operational purposes.
