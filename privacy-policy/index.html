Legal & Data Safety

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Application: SplitUdhar (Android App: com.techwebcode.splitudhar) • Developer: TechWebCode

At SplitUdhar (developed and operated by TechWebCode, accessible at splitudhar.techwebcode.in), we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, handle, store, and safeguard your data when you use the SplitUdhar mobile application and website.

🛡️
Commitment to User Privacy We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers. Your financial records and contact details are used strictly to provide and improve the SplitUdhar expense-tracking service.

1. Information We Collect

To provide personal expense tracking, group splitting, and account synchronization, we collect the following types of information:

A. Account and Profile Information

  • Registration Details: When you sign up or authenticate using Google Sign-In or your email address, we collect your name, email address, and optional profile image URL.
  • Authentication Data: Secure authentication tokens to keep your session logged in safely without storing plaintext passwords on our servers.

B. Transaction & Expense Data

  • Expense Records: Amounts, transaction descriptions/titles, transaction dates, category tags, split allocations, settlement records, and notes you manually create.
  • Group Details: Group names, member lists, and group-level expense apportionments created by you or your group participants.

C. Contacts Information (Optional Permission)

  • With your explicit runtime permission (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS), the SplitUdhar app allows you to select friends from your device contacts book to easily add them to an expense or group without typing their details manually.
  • On-Device Access: Your address book is accessed on your device solely for selecting specific contacts. We do not upload or store your entire contact list on our servers. Only the names or identifiers of the specific individuals you choose to add to an expense or group are stored with your transaction records.

D. Technical, Device and Diagnostic Data

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and language settings.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens required to deliver real-time notifications when an expense is added or settled.
  • Log and Crash Reports: Anonymous crash traces, error reports, and performance diagnostics collected via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs and improve application reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Core Service Functionality: Calculating group expense balances, determining who owes whom, tracking lending and borrowing, and recording settlements.
  • Multi-Device Sync: Securely synchronizing your transactions, groups, and friend ledgers across your logged-in devices via our cloud API (api.techwebcode.in).
  • Notifications & Alerts: Sending you updates when a friend records an expense, settles a payment, or invites you to a group.
  • App Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting your account from unauthorized access and preventing abuse.
  • Customer Support: Responding to inquiries, feedback, and account deletion requests submitted to our support team.

3. Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

SplitUdhar integrates select industry-standard third-party SDKs to facilitate authentication, diagnostics, and notifications:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: For secure single sign-on authentication and app update management (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Firebase: Including Firebase Core, Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics (crash diagnostics).
  • Google Mobile Ads: SplitUdhar may display non-intrusive advertisements to support the free tier of the application. Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous advertising IDs and device diagnostics in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Cloudflare & Cloud Hosting: Secure API infrastructure and content delivery network (CDN) ensuring encrypted transit and high availability.

4. Data Storage and Security Measures

We employ robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the SplitUdhar mobile app, website, and backend servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Biometric Lock Support: The app includes optional local biometric/PIN authentication to safeguard your expense records on your mobile device against unauthorized physical access.
  • Secure Server Access: Database records are maintained in restricted-access cloud environments with automated security monitoring and periodic security reviews.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain your information only as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the SplitUdhar service.

You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • Direct In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account Permanently to delete your profile, transaction ledgers, and credentials immediately.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the app, you can request full account and data deletion via our dedicated Account Deletion Page or by emailing support@techwebcode.in.

Upon processing your deletion request, your account data, profile details, and personal transaction records are permanently purged from our active databases. Backups are rotated and cleared within standard operational cycles (maximum 30 days).

6. Children's Privacy

SplitUdhar is intended for general audiences who manage personal and shared expenses. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at support@techwebcode.in and we will expeditiously remove such data.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features, operational practices, or legal requirements. When updates occur, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
